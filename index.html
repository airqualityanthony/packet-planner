    Where to get UK terrain

    • OS Terrain 50 — free, 50 m, whole of GB, ASCII grid
    • Environment Agency LIDAR — 1–2 m DTM, England
    • Scottish Remote Sensing Portal — LIDAR DTM, Scotland
    • DataMapWales — LIDAR DTM, Wales
    • OpenDataNI — terrain for Northern Ireland
    • USGS EarthExplorer — SRTM 1 arc-second (.hgt), anywhere

    Use DTM (bare earth) rather than DSM unless you want buildings and trees treated as solid.
